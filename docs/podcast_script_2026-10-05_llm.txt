Forsythia polysaccharides (FPs) protect mice from dextran sulfate sodium‑induced colitis by reshaping gut microbiota metabolism and the PI3K/AKT signaling cascade. In the study, FP treatment reduced disease activity scores, restored colon length and lowered inflammatory cytokines. Mechanistically, FPs decreased microbiota‑derived palmitic acid levels, which in turn attenuated PI3K/AKT activation in colonic epithelial cells, leading to diminished NF‑κB‑driven inflammation. The authors conclude that targeting microbial metabolites with natural polysaccharides offers a promising strategy for inflammatory bowel disease. Source: PubMed — International journal of biological macromolecules

Age-related shifts in the salivary microbiome are linked to increased cariogenic potential, according to a cross‑sectional study published in the Journal of Dentistry via PubMed. Researchers recruited 229 participants between February 2022 and September 2025 and compared saliva‑derived microbial profiles of older and younger adults. They found that the older cohort displayed a distinct salivary microbiome characterized by reduced diversity and enrichment of bacteria associated with dental caries, which correlated with higher caries risk scores. These findings highlight the salivary microbiome as an age‑dependent marker for caries susceptibility and suggest microbiome‑targeted strategies for oral health in aging populations.

Indigenous microbiota drives divergent wine flavors even when grapes come from the same vineyard, according to a study in the International Journal of Food Microbiology. Researchers found that the spontaneous fermentation of grape must is shaped primarily by the host grape variety, with microbial consortia varying significantly between varieties and producing distinct metabolic profiles that underlie wine typicity. Annual vintage conditions had a comparatively weaker influence on the fermenting ecosystem. The work highlights how vineyard‑scale microbiome differences, rooted in grape variety, can translate directly into sensory differences in the final wine.
