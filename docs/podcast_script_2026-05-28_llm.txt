By offering a pragmatic, manually annotated set of terms, microntology enables consistent tagging of samples across datasets, facilitating comparative analyses and meta‑studies without the overhead of larger ontologies. The work highlights how a focused vocabulary can improve data integration and reproducibility in microbiome research.
