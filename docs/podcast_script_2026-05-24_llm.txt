Researchers have uncovered a set of shared downstream genes that are co‑regulated by the biofilm transcription factors Wor3 and Bcr1 in Candida albicans, revealing common anti‑biofilm effectors within the pathogen’s large transcriptional network. Mutations in either Wor3 or Bcr1 produce the same severe biofilm‑defective phenotype across multiple clinical isolates, indicating that these regulators converge on a core set of virulence genes. By identifying these shared targets, the study points to potential molecular choke points for disrupting C. albicans biofilms and reducing its pathogenic impact. Source: PubMed — Genetics.
