The authors map mediating mechanisms—such as short‑chain fatty acid production, bile‑acid transformation and barrier reinforcement—while noting critical evidence gaps: limited human trials, inconsistent dosing, and insufficient structural‑activity data. They call for standardized polysaccharide characterization and longitudinal studies to clarify causality and therapeutic potential.

Beyond pathogen recognition: C-type lectin receptors in microbiota regulation. PubMed – Mucosal immunology reports that intestinal immune cells do not rely on a single receptor to decide whether to tolerate or attack microbes; instead, C-type lectin receptors (CLRs) integrate signals from multiple pattern recognition receptors to shape responses to the resident microbiota. This integrative signaling allows CLRs to distinguish commensals from pathogens and to modulate microbial communities, extending their role beyond innate pathogen sensing to active microbiota regulation. The study highlights how PRR crosstalk, mediated by CLRs, underpins intestinal homeostasis.
