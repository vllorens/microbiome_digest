While short‑chain fatty acids and bile acids dominate current axis models, they fail to capture lipid‑driven pathways that contribute to cognitive decline. The review in Comprehensive Physiology (PubMed) highlights how gut‑derived oxysterols can travel to the liver and brain, modulating inflammation, cholesterol metabolism, and neuronal survival, offering a mechanistic link between microbiome alterations and neurodegenerative diseases. This perspective expands the axis beyond traditional metabolites, positioning oxysterols as key mediators of systemic and neural health.
