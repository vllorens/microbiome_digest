Using comparative genetics across several plant species, the authors show that disrupting CSSP alters microbial community structure in ways that differ between hosts, indicating that a universal symbiosis pathway can be tuned to produce distinct microbiome assemblies. This links a core symbiosis system to host‑tailored root‑associated communities. Source: bioRxiv — plant biology preprint.
