[[TRANSITION]]

MetagenomicKG delivers a comprehensive knowledge graph designed specifically for metagenomic research, addressing the challenge of navigating the immense diversity and volume of microbial genomic data. By integrating taxonomic hierarchies from the Genome Taxonomy Database, functional annotations from the Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes, and additional pathway and phenotype resources, the graph provides a single, queryable framework that links organisms to their genes, metabolites, and ecological roles. This unified structure enables researchers to perform cross‑domain analyses—such as linking community composition to metabolic potential—more efficiently than using disparate databases separately.
